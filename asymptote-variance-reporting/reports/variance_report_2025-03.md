## Limitations

- Synthetic data only — variances, shocks and forecasts demonstrate the pipeline, not real firm performance.
- Forecast intervals assume stable residual volatility; a regime change or large redemption would break them.
- Anomaly detection is unsupervised on 24 monthly points — small sample, so WATCH items need human review, not automatic action.
