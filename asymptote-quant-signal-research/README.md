# Asymptote Quant Signal Research — Signal Discovery & Validation Pipeline

**A signal discovery + validation pipeline built for the Quantitative Researcher
Intern role at Asymptote Capital (New York, NY, $90–100/hr, apply by Nov 18, 2026).**

> ⚠️ **ALL DATA IS SYNTHETIC.** Prices, signals, ICs, Sharpes and backtest
> results are generated by code (seed 2026). Tickers are prefixed `SYN_` so
> nothing can be mistaken for a real security or real market data. This is a
> demonstration of the *research process* — signal discovery, statistical
> testing, multiple-testing control, purged walk-forward backtesting, and
> research documentation — not a trading strategy, not production trading,
> and not evidence of a real edge.

## Executive summary

- **17 candidate signals** in 5 families are tested against 5-day forward
  returns on 12 synthetic assets over 1,512 trading days (2020–2025).
- **Headline result (honest): NO signal survives all validation gates.**
  Best IC is 0.045 (MOM_120d); best Benjamini-Hochberg p-value is 0.057
  (above the 5% FDR bar); best deflated Sharpe is 0.63 (bar: 0.95).
- The pipeline's value is the refusal: it correctly declines to certify
  weak effects, while still showing *where* the weak evidence points
  (long-horizon momentum) and *how unstable* it is across folds.
- Deliverables: runnable pipeline (`run.py`), 11 unit tests, 6 charts,
  a research memo, a model report, 2 notebooks, and a 10-slide deck.

## 2. ML problem definition (Step 2)

| Problem | Input | Output | Target | Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 — Signal discovery | 17 daily z-scored signals × 12 SYN_ assets | Ranked signal list by IC | 5-day forward log return | Ranking / regression (IC) |
| P2 — Statistical validation | Daily cross-sectional IC series per signal | NW t-stat, corrected p-values, deflated Sharpe | Is the IC distinguishable from noise after 17 trials? | Hypothesis testing |
| P3 — Out-of-sample evaluation | Purged walk-forward folds | Test IC, test Sharpe, decay per fold | Does the signal survive unseen time? | Time-series validation |

Constraints: interpretability over complexity (no black box as the hero);
no look-ahead (signal at close *t* predicts returns after *t*); every claim
must survive three independent gates (FDR, deflated Sharpe, walk-forward).

## 3. Data + model strategy (Steps 3–4)

**Data schema:** `dates (1,512) × 12 SYN_ prices` → 17 signal columns
(z-scored daily) → `fwd_5d` log return. Full sample in
`data/synthetic/syn_prices.csv`; validation tables in `data/processed/`.

**Synthetic ground truth (known to the generator, not the validator):**
two weak true effects — 60-day momentum (0.035σ) and 5-day reversal
(0.025σ) — plus a common market factor, idiosyncratic noise, and two stress
episodes (Jun 2022 bear, Mar 2024 vol spike). Everything else is noise by
design. A method that "discovers" a dozen strong signals here is broken;
a good method finds weak momentum evidence and little else.

**Signal families (17):** Momentum — MOM_5/20/60/120d, MOM_12m1;
Mean reversion — REV_5/20d, REV_MA60; Volatility — VOL_20d, VOL_ratio,
VOL_absret; Trend — TREND_MA20, TREND_MACD, TREND_breakout;
Cross-sectional — XSEC_RSI14, XSEC_skew20, XSEC_ret1d.

**Models:** no classifier is the hero. Baselines are the noise signals
themselves (the control group). Evaluation: Spearman IC, Newey-West t
(lag 5), Bonferroni/Holm/BH corrections, deflated Sharpe (Bailey &
Lopez de Prado, annualised-Sharpe form — T in years, not days), and a
long-short tercile diagnostic net of 5 bps turnover costs.

**Overfitting controls:** pre-registered 17-signal candidate set (no
adding variants after seeing results); purged walk-forward (5-day purge +
5-day embargo, 3 expanding folds); signal-correlation clustering to count
independent bets, not signals.

Business insight (for a non-ML hiring manager): the desk's risk is not
missing a signal — it is sizing a phantom one. Three independent gates
all saying "not yet" is a result, and it is worth more than a backtest
Sharpe of 5 on the same data.

| MOM_60d | 0.0422 | 2.71 | 0.0067 | 0.0567 | 0.94 | 0.63 |
| TREND_MACD | 0.0350 | 2.14 | 0.0324 | 0.1100 | 0.60 | 0.37 |

\* Long-short top/bottom tercile, 1-day hold, net of 5 bps turnover costs.
**BH-FDR 5% survivors: NONE.** Best BH p = 0.057 (MOM_60d / MOM_120d).
Max deflated Sharpe 0.63 vs the 0.95 bar. The embedded 5-day reversal
(0.025σ) was *not* detected (REV_5d IC −0.007, p 0.62) — correctly: it is
too weak for this sample size, and the pipeline says so instead of
inventing a finding.

Walk-forward (3 folds, purged + embargo; top signals, mean test IC / Sharpe):

| Signal | Test IC f1 | Test IC f2 | Test IC f3 | Test Sharpe f1 | Test Sharpe f2 | Test Sharpe f3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MOM_120d | 0.083 | 0.084 | 0.029 | 2.34 | 1.21 | 0.13 |
| MOM_60d | 0.030 | 0.114 | 0.051 | 1.43 | 2.29 | 0.90 |
| TREND_MACD | 0.011 | 0.122 | 0.048 | 0.68 | 2.41 | −0.16 |
| TREND_breakout | 0.058 | 0.069 | 0.054 | 1.92 | 1.85 | 0.67 |

Read: test ICs are positive in every fold for the momentum family, but
fold Sharpe swings from 2.3 to 0.13 — one fold would have "proved" the
signal and another would have killed it. That variance *is* the finding.

## 10. Differentiation layer (Step 10)

**What differentiates this from 99% of candidates' projects?**
1. The headline result is a *refusal* — most portfolio projects report a
   backtested Sharpe and stop; this one runs three independent gates and
   reports that nothing passes them.
2. The ground truth is known and disclosed: two weak effects were embedded
   on purpose, so the validator can be scored against reality — and the
   reversal effect was correctly *not* found.
3. The deflated Sharpe is implemented in annualised form (T in years);
   the days-based form — the common implementation error — would have
   fabricated significance ~16× over. That correction is documented in code.

**Insights that would impress a hiring manager:**
- The 17 signals are ~6 independent bets (VOL pair correlates 0.99,
  MOM/REV pair −0.87) — counting signals overstates the search, and the
  deflated Sharpe must use the *effective* trial count.
- Test IC exceeding train IC in folds 1–2 is not "no overfitting" — with
  fold Sharpe swinging 2.3 → 0.13, the honest read is high variance, not
  a stable edge.
- A BH p of 0.057 is a *near miss*, not a pass — and the memo says so
  rather than rounding the bar down.

**5 questions to ask in the interview:**
1. How does the desk currently control for multiple testing across researchers?
2. What is the pre-registration process for a new signal family?
3. Which frictions (borrow, capacity, impact) dominate your cost model?
4. How many independent bets does the current signal library really contain?
5. What would make you *kill* a signal that looks good in-sample?

## Limitations (stated openly)

- Synthetic data with only two embedded true effects; real markets have
  regimes, jumps, and microstructure this generator lacks.
- 12-asset cross-section; IC estimates are noisy; 3 walk-forward folds only.
- Cost model is turnover × 5 bps, not a fill simulator.
- No production deployment, no live trading, no real-market claims, no
  PhD-level credential claimed.
